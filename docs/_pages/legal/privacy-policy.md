# Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 19:10 on Aug 5, 2026

Swihomo is a native client for iOS and macOS. This Privacy Policy explains
what information the app handles and how responsibility is shared when you
use third-party services through the app.

## No Personal Information Collection

Swihomo does not collect, sell, or share personal information with the app
developer. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Swihomo does not operate a developer-controlled analytics, advertising,
tracking, or personal-data collection service.

## Information Stored Locally

To provide its features, Swihomo may store the following information locally
on your device or in the app's sandbox:

- Configuration files and YAML content that you import or create.
- Profile names, subscription URLs, custom User-Agent values, and related
  profile metadata that you provide.
- App preferences and connection settings.
- Application and mihomo core logs generated while you use the app.

This information is used to operate the app and is not sent to the developer
by Swihomo. You are responsible for managing or deleting this information on
your device.

## Third-Party Services

Swihomo can connect to third-party services that you configure, including
proxy providers, DNS providers, subscription URLs, and other network
endpoints. Those services may receive, process, transmit, or collect
information according to their own policies and terms.

Swihomo does not control and is not responsible for the privacy practices,
security, availability, or data handling of third-party services.

## User Responsibility

You are responsible for the information and configurations you add to
Swihomo, the third-party services you choose to use, and the network traffic
generated by your configuration and actions.

Swihomo is not responsible for third-party transmission, processing, or
collection of information caused by your use of the app, your configuration,
or your interaction with third-party services.

Please review the privacy policies and terms of each third-party service
before using it.

## Data Security

Swihomo is designed to keep app-managed information in the app's local
sandbox. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and you
are responsible for protecting your device, configurations, credentials, and
network access.

## Children's Privacy

Swihomo does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you
believe that a child has provided personal information to the developer,
please contact us using the information below.

## Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be
published on this page with an updated date.
